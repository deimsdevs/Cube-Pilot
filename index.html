Learn

The pieces

A cube has 6 centers (they never move and define each face's color), 12 edges (2 colors each) and 8 corners (3 colors each).

Holding the cube

Hold it with the white center on top and the green center facing you. The red center is then on your right.

Notation

R, L, U, D, F, B are the Right, Left, Up, Down, Front and Back faces. A letter alone means a quarter turn clockwise, looking straight at that face. R' (prime) is a quarter turn counterclockwise. R2 is a half turn.

Common mistakes

  • Turning counterclockwise when the move says clockwise. Always look straight at the face being turned.
  • Holding the cube differently from the screen. Match the white-top, green-front view first.
  • Entering the Down or Back face upside down.
  • Making one wrong turn and carrying on. Stop, re-enter your cube, and solve again.

A step-by-step beginner layer-by-layer course is not part of this version. Use Solve for a complete solution.

Help

Orientation: white center on top, green center facing you, red center on your right. Each face is read looking straight at it. The Up face has the Back edge at the top, the Down face has the Front edge at the top, and the side faces have the Up face at the top.

Notation: R, L, U, D, F, B turn that face clockwise as you look straight at it. A prime (R') means counterclockwise. A 2 (R2) means a half turn.

Controls: drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click the cube to reset the view.
